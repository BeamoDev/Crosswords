## Modes, generation and queue authority

**When the user says Classic, they mean the current Standard queue.** Queue shows `Classic Mode (Ns)`; internal mode/request keys remain `Standard`. The old `Classic` generator mode is retired and still rejects.

Grid dimensions below are **columns x rows**.

| Mode | Unlock | Board | Admission/rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard / displayed Classic | Any level | 9x9/7 words below ten solves; 12x12/10-12 afterward | Private below 10 saved solves, shared afterward; 15-second queues |
| Mini | Level 5 | 6x6; 5 short words; east/south | Random solo board directly from Home, no queue |
| Jumbo | Level 10 | 16x16; 20 words; all eight directions | Random solo board directly from Home, no queue |
| Hardcore | Level 20 | 12x12; 12 longer words; all eight directions | Solo; one validated wrong selection ends the round |
| Duel | Existing challenge flow | 11x11; 10 words | 1v1 countdown/race protocol and dedicated server service |
| Timed | No current launcher | 12x12 definition and title/panel support | Retained presentation/definition only; no implemented timed gameplay rules |

- Unlocks have one authority: `ProgressionConfig.Experience.ModeLevels = { Mini = 5, Jumbo = 10, Hardcore = 20 }`. Both GameController and QueueService read it. Do not hardcode a second set of gates.
- Classic below ten saved solves uses 9x9, seven familiar 3-6-letter words and E/S/SE directions. At ten solves it switches to 12x12 with 10-12 themed words and E/S/SE/SW directions. The beginner board has 43.75% fewer cells; difficulty reduction is approximate. BuildClassicDefinition applies this policy to private queues and Queue.Solo/replays from authoritative saved solves. Placement retries preserve dimensions; skill averaging is not used. Saved solves below 10 still select private companion admission; completion advances solves.
- Graduates share one canonical Classic board. Human skill and bot levels no longer change the board format.
- PuzzleCatalog has 34 full theme pools plus five separate curated BeginnerThemes pools. Roblox, Brainrot, Obbies, Tycoons, Pet Collecting, Football, Basketball and Racing join the original pools, and Sports is expanded. Each new pack qualifies for all six supported definitions after length filtering. Keep packs in the existing seeded rotation; no pack selector was added.
- `PuzzleGenerator` uses independent row/column bounds for placement, candidate starts and filler. `gridSize` remains the row count; optional `gridWidth` supplies columns and defaults to `gridSize` for older square definitions. Generated letters and metadata must agree. Standard dimensions remain fixed across placement retries. BoardController derives its aspect from the actual letters matrix.
- The first queued member (real player or bot) starts the 15-second server deadline. Later joins share that deadline; duplicate joins do not add members or restart it. Removing the first member does not reset the timer while others remain. Empty queues cancel.
- On expiry, detach the cohort, reset the published waiting count, generate one canonical puzzle and distribute it to those members. New arrivals start another queue, even while prior cohorts are playing. A one-person queue still starts normally. Two queued players do not automatically become a Duel.
- Each member has independent found/hinted words and completion. Leaving removes only that member and their finder portraits; dispose of the round after its last real member leaves and release its bots to requeue. Active rounds are session-local and do not resume across rejoining the server.
- `GameConfig.Bots = { Enabled = true, ServerSize = 12, MinBots = 2 }` enables Standard queue bots. One public server-wide pool draws from 2 through `max(0, 12 - realPlayerCount)` bots (minimum clamped to available slots), rerolling for the next cohort, on population changes and every 60-120 seconds; empty/full servers admit no new bots, while existing round opponents stay until the humans finish. Bots become due after 2-3 seconds, then join one at a time with a randomized gap budgeted against the remaining countdown and pending bots, never shorter than 0.45 seconds. Slow ticks and batches of ready avatars cannot cause catch-up bursts; normal queue counts/deadlines remain shared. Bot-only cohorts recycle after 1-3 seconds without generating puzzles. Standard queue bots never enter solo, Duel or tutorial practice; Duel uses its own session-only pool.
- Bots have generated names/levels, reserved session IDs starting at -1000000000, `isBot = true` and a server-resolved `thumbnailImage` from a random Roblox account. After four unsuccessful thumbnail attempts, use only a previously verified headshot (cache capped at 64). With no verified image yet, retry after 10-20 seconds and keep that bot out of the queue. Never use a hardcoded account/default-profile fallback or the simulated ID for a lookup. Both authored queue cards and finder portraits use that image at ImageTransparency 0 and hide an optional authored Profile placeholder overlay. Bot finds use canonical paths and adapt to human progress as described below, without any XP, inventory, statistics, leaderboard or DataStore writes. Population changes only retire idle/queued bots. Active bots stay until every real member finishes or exits, even if the server fills. Finishing releases bots for reuse while retaining final portraits in the completed round snapshot. Release checks the expected round so exiting an old board cannot interrupt a bot in its next round. QueueService.Stop stops scheduling at shutdown.
- Bots use varied Roblox-style handles (plain names, prefixes, suffixes, underscores and optional short numbers), unique within the active pool. Each round observes every human's accepted-word count and recent solving intervals. After at most one opening find, most bots trail the median active human progress by 0-2 words; 35% of public rounds designate at most one challenger, capped one word ahead of the leading active human. A challenger can finish only when a human has reached the last word; other bots cannot finish before a human. First attempts wait 18-40 seconds, later delays adapt to measured pace, word length, individual variation and occasional pauses. A shared 2-5-second gap prevents simultaneous bot solves, including after slow ticks. Idle humans cannot cause a bot to rush through the board; bots keep their existing round until its humans finish or leave. These are simulated companions, with no profile/XP/reward writes.
- Each beginner has a private QueueState/QueueBots pair, preparing 2-11 companions independently of public capacity (including full servers). Unique private IDs start below -2000000000; levels range 1 through owner level + 2. Only verified ready portraits enter before the normal deadline; missing images never delay the board. No beginner challenger may finish first. Freeze private lobby admission during play, preserve final portraits, and dispose private ownership on Exit/cancel/disconnect/generation failure/shutdown. ProfileReady/RoundBusy/DuelBusy/TutorialBusy gates apply to private queues too.
- QueueUpdated uses per-recipient FireClient: beginners see only their private roster/count/deadline, graduates see public waiting. Publication revisions increase across both streams; do not revert to independent queue revisions or leak another private roster. Reuse the authored Main.Queue and existing round protocol.
- Each accepted canonical word find records member.foundOrder[word] using the increasing server round revision. Snapshots copy this map. BoardController sorts each word's finders by that value, earliest on the left, displaying the earliest three remaining solvers and +X afterward. A departed finder promotes the next solver; duplicates, hints and roster reordering cannot replace the accepted solve order. Legacy/tutorial snapshots without an order map retain roster order as a fallback.
- The server generates and retains the canonical board; word requests are checked against its stored paths, not a client clear or found-word snapshot. QueueState accepts forward or reversed traversal of the canonical path. Duplicate/invalid finds grant no XP or completion reward.
- Hardcore failure requires an actual contiguous, straight, in-bounds wrong selection of at least two cells. Empty, bent, skipped-cell or off-board payloads do not count as a loss. Canonical/repeated/reversed valid words do not lose. Failed/completed rounds cannot award another outcome.
- Preserve `ProfileReady`, `RoundBusy`, `DuelBusy` and `TutorialBusy` gates. Queues, solo rounds, tutorial sessions and Duel must not overlap.
- Standard/Mini/Jumbo/Hardcore have **no solve deadline or speed-based reward**. The queue timer and intro animation are presentation/admission, not a loss timer. Daily login reset is a meta system; playtime rewards are removed. Do not restore normal-mode timer products, freezes, extensions or speed bonuses.
